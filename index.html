Browse. Find. Save.

Vidkap is a web browser with its own video download manager. Find the videos on the pages you visit, save the ones you're allowed to keep and watch them offline.

Finds videos on the pageMP4, WebM, HLS and DASH, listed with length, quality and file size.
Pick the quality1080p, 720p or a smaller file. Streams are saved as standard MP4.
Download managerQueue, live progress, cancel and one-tap retry. Notifications when done.
Offline librarySearch, sort, share and play your videos with the built-in player.
Private by designNo accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking. Your data stays on your device.
English and TurkishLight and dark themes. iPhone, iPad and Android.

Use it responsibly

Only download videos you own, videos with an open license (such as Creative Commons or public domain) or videos you have permission to save. Vidkap never downloads DRM-protected content and does not support downloading from YouTube and certain other services.
